About me

Abdallah Helal

I'm an AI automation engineer. I build the unglamorous half of this work: the webhooks, the error handling, the retries. The parts that decide whether a workflow survives real traffic.

A workflow that only works when the API is healthy is not finished. So I design systems that break on purpose and recover on their own, then hand clients something they can run without me.

I serve companies across MENA and internationally, from clinics to real-estate firms to SaaS teams. And I teach the same work in Arabic to thousands on YouTube.
